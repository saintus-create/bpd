# Mental health knowledge base (sample)

Sample documents used to demonstrate the bpd voice assistant's retrieval.

> These are **demonstration documents**. They contain general information
> written for a product demo. They are **not medical advice**, not a
> diagnosis, and not a substitute for care from a qualified professional.

## Contents

- `overview.md` — what mental health covers, and how to think about it
- `anxiety.md` — anxiety, panic and when to get help
- `depression.md` — low mood and depression
- `treatment-options.md` — talking therapies, medication and self-care
- `clinical-trials.md` — what trials are, and how to find real ones
- `crisis-support.md` — what to do in a crisis, and helplines

The voice assistant searches every `.md` file in `knowledge/` and returns the
passages that best match the question.
