# Crisis support

> Sample document for the bpd demo. In an emergency, contact your local
> emergency number.

## If you are in immediate danger

Call your local emergency number. In India that is **112**.

If you can, stay with someone, or ask someone to stay with you, until help
arrives. Move away from anything you could use to hurt yourself.

## Helplines

### India

- **Tele-MANAS** — **14416** (or 1-800-891-4416). Free, 24/7, in multiple
  Indian languages. The national tele-mental health programme.
- **KIRAN Helpline** — **1800-599-0019**. Free, 24/7, in 13 languages.
- **AASRA** — **+91-9820466726**. 24/7 befriending service.
- **Emergency** — **112**.

### Elsewhere

- **US and Canada** — call or text **988** (Suicide and Crisis Lifeline).
- **UK and Ireland** — **116 123** (Samaritans), free, 24/7.
- **Australia** — **13 11 14** (Lifeline).
- **International directory** — the International Association for Suicide
  Prevention lists crisis centres by country at iasp.info.

## If you are worried about someone else

- Ask directly whether they are thinking about suicide. Asking does not plant
  the idea, and people usually feel relief at being asked.
- Stay with them, or make sure someone else can.
- Help them contact a helpline or a clinician, and offer to make the call with
  them.
- Remove or lock away means of harm where that is possible.
- Follow up afterwards.

## What crisis support is not

A helpline is not a diagnosis, and it does not replace ongoing care. It is
there to get someone through the immediate moment and connect them to
something more sustained.
