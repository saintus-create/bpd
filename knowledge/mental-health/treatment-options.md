# Treatment options

> Sample document for the bpd demo. General information only — not medical
> advice.

## Talking therapies

- **Cognitive behavioural therapy (CBT)** — examines the link between
  thoughts, feelings and behaviour. The most studied therapy for anxiety and
  depression.
- **Behavioural activation** — rebuilds activity and routine during
  depression.
- **Interpersonal therapy (IPT)** — focuses on relationships and role
  transitions.
- **EMDR and trauma-focused CBT** — used for post-traumatic stress.
- **Counselling and psychotherapy** — a broader space to talk, useful for many
  difficulties.

Therapy is usually delivered weekly, in courses of six to twenty sessions,
individually or in groups, in person or remotely.

## Medication

Different classes are used for different conditions — SSRIs and SNRIs for
depression and anxiety, for example. Medication is prescribed by a clinician,
takes weeks to work, and is usually continued for a period after recovery
rather than stopped at the first good week. Side effects and interactions
should be discussed with the prescriber.

## Self-care that has evidence behind it

- Consistent sleep and wake times
- Regular movement, even walking
- Daylight exposure, particularly in the morning
- Reducing alcohol and other depressants
- Staying connected to at least one or two people
- Limiting rumination — scheduled worry time rather than all-day

Self-care supports treatment. It is not a replacement for it when symptoms are
significant.

## Choosing what to try

Treatment is individual. Factors include the specific condition, how severe it
is, previous responses, other health conditions, cost, and what is available
locally. It is reasonable to ask a clinician what the options are, what the
evidence is, and what to do if the first approach does not help.

## Where to start

A general practitioner, a community mental health service, or a licensed
therapist are the usual routes. In India, the National Tele-Mental Health
Programme (Tele-MANAS, 14416) offers free telephone support — see
`crisis-support.md`.
